*Stappenplan schrijven*


1. **Read the assignment.** Who are you writing to? Why are you writing? What kind of text is needed? Choose informal language (jij/je, jouw) or formal language (u, uw).
2. **Read the tips beside the exercise.** Use their examples while writing.
3. **Write a draft.**
4. **Check your draft.** Work through every tip and mark it when checked.
5. **Correct your text.** Read your teacher’s feedback and make corrections.
6. **Write the final version** in your workbook.
