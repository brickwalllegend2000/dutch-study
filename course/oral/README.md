## 2. Schriftelijk (S) & Mondeling (M) Overlaps: What You Must Know

While your immediate evaluation may be the **written exam (Schrijven)**, *Waystage 1.2* treats language acquisition as an integrated whole: the oral track trains automaticity in spoken sentence frames, which directly transfer to written composition.

### Why Oral Mastery Powers Written Success

1. **Word order is identical:** Spoken Dutch strictly enforces the second-position verb rule in main clauses, inversion after fronted time/place adverbs (*Nu ben ik...*, *Daarna hebben we...*), and verb-final order in subordinate clauses (*omdat...*, *om ... te + inf*). Practicing these aloud eliminates inversion errors in writing.
2. **Prepositional accuracy:** Whether describing a room on paper ([Exam Prompt 2](../exams/exam-preparation.md#2-describe-a-home-or-room-woning--favoriete-kamer)) or directing a mover aloud ([Modeling 5](modeling-05.md)), the distinction between action verbs (*zetten/leggen/hangen*) and position verbs (*staan/liggen/hangen*) is identical.
3. **Conversational formulas are written templates:** Spoken telephone apologies (*Het spijt me, maar ik kan niet komen want...*) become the opening sentences of written excuse emails ([Exam Prompt 3](../exams/exam-preparation.md#3-write-a-personal-message-or-email-persoonlijk-berichtje)).

## 3. High-Yield Exam Checklist

Whether sitting the written test or taking the oral interview, keep these four operational rules at the forefront:

1. **Verb Second (De persoonsvorm op plaats 2):**
   - Normaal: *Ik woon sinds drie jaar in Gent.*
   - Inversie (na tijd of plaats): *Sinds drie jaar **woon** ik in Gent.*
2. **Subordinating Conjunctions push verbs to the end:**
   - *..., want ik **moet** werken.* (Nevenschikking — normale volgorde)
   - *..., omdat ik **moet werken**.* (Onderschikking — alle werkwoorden achteraan)
3. **Purpose clauses with om ... te + infinitief:**
   - *Ik bel **om** een afspraak **te maken**.*
   - *Ik neem de fiets **om** files **te vermijden**.*
4. **Adjective inflection:**
   - De grote man, de zwarte broek, het mooie kleedje.
   - Maar: *een **mooi** kleedje* (geen *-e* bij *een/geen* + *het*-woord).
