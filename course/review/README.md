# Review sheets

[Home](../../README.md) · [Chapters](../README.md) · [Exercises](../exercises/README.md) · [Cheat sheets](../../wiki/cheat-sheets/README.md)

One page per written chapter for revising before a test: key vocabulary, the grammar to know, what to watch out for, and the chapter's can-do goals. Each sheet is compiled from the chapter guide, `vocabulary.csv`, the maintained grammar notes and the chapter exercises. Nothing here has been reviewed.

- Examples marked † were written for the sheets to show a rule or a slip. They are not from the course and are unverified.
- The *Watch out* lists are derived from the course rules and writing tips; the course itself has no list of common mistakes.
- The vocabulary lines were copied from `vocabulary.csv` on 2026-10-23. The generated [chapter vocabulary lists](../../wiki/README.md) stay authoritative; update a sheet if its words change.
