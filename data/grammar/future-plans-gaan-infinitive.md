*Gaan + infinitief*

[Grammar guide](../../sources/pages/401f89e5e0fc819da383d5825749b371.md)

Use a form of **gaan** followed by an infinitive to describe a plan, as in the city-trip exercise.

## Pattern

**Subject + gaan + other information + infinitive.**

- **Ik ga winkelen.** — I’m going to go shopping.
- **Wij gaan winkelen.** — We’re going to go shopping.
- **Morgen gaan we winkelen.** — Tomorrow we’re going shopping. This last sentence is supplied in the archive.

## Choose the right form

- **ik ga**
- **jij/je gaat, u gaat, hij/zij gaat**
- **wij/we, jullie, zij/ze gaan**

In a question with jij/je after the verb, drop the -t: **Ga je winkelen?**

When **morgen** comes first, swap the verb and subject: **Morgen gaan we …**. Keep the infinitive at the end. Use the same pattern for the archive’s unfinished Mercator prompt; the missing photo means its exact intended answer cannot be recovered.

## Try it

1. Wij … morgen … . (gaan + winkelen)
2. Begin your answer to 1 with **Morgen**.
3. Ask a friend whether they are going shopping: **… je … ?**

Answers

1. Wij **gaan** morgen **winkelen**.
2. **Morgen gaan wij winkelen.**
3. **Ga je winkelen?**
