[Original Notion page](https://app.notion.com/p/401f89e5e0fc81a68f04fe468b0ed51a?pvs=204) · [Exact retrieval payload](../notion/401f89e5e0fc81a68f04fe468b0ed51a.json)

**Goal:** Describe three lost objects on a lost-property form.
**Time:** 20 minutes · **Output:** at least two sentences per object

## 1. Your task

Je bent drie voorwerpen kwijt. Vul het formulier in voor elk voorwerp. Schrijf minimum twee zinnen bij elke beschrijving. Begin de eerste zin met *Het is* of *Het zijn*.

### Words to use

| Type | Words |
| --- | --- |
| Objects | de sjaal, de sporttas, de handtas, de portemonnee, de paraplu, de handdoek, de sleutel |
| Details | de rits, de knoop, de streep |
| Patterns | effen, gestreept, geruit, gebloemd |
| Materials | wollen, katoenen |
| Verbs | vergeten, vinden, terugvinden, ophalen, invullen |

### Grammar checklist

- [ ] First sentence starts with *Het is* or *Het zijn*
- [ ] Adjective endings: *een groene wollen sjaal*, *een zwarte sporttas*
- [ ] A subject and a verb in every sentence
- [ ] *met* or *zonder* for details

## 3. Check your forms

- [ ] Each description has at least two sentences.
- [ ] Each description starts with *Het is* or *Het zijn*.
- [ ] Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with punctuation.
- [ ] Adjective endings are correct.
- [ ] You checked the spelling of difficult words.
